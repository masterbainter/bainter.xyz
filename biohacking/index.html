Comparative Synthesis

Evolutionary / Strict Carnivore

**Core Philosophy:** Elimination is key. Humans evolved to eat meat; plants contain defense chemicals (antinutrients) that cause inflammation and brain fog.

  • ➤ Focus Hack: Stable blood sugar and ketone production (without fasting) leads to "unshakeable" mental clarity.
  • ➤ Key Figures: Shawn Baker, Jordan Peterson, Ken Berry.
  • ➤ Supplements: Minimalist. Salt, electrolytes, organ meats (for some).

Integrative / Data-Driven

**Core Philosophy:** Additive optimization. Use technology, diverse nutrients, and supplements to upgrade biology beyond baseline survival.

  • ➤ Focus Hack: Circadian regulation (light), Nootropics, intermittent fasting, and micronutrient density.
  • ➤ Key Figures: Andrew Huberman, Dave Asprey, Bryan Johnson.
  • ➤ Supplements: Extensive. Omega-3s, Vitamin D, Magnesium, specific peptides.
